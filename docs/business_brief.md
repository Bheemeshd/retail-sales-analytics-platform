# Business brief

## Context

A multichannel retailer needs one trusted view of commercial performance across stores, web, mobile and marketplace sales. Leadership currently sees revenue totals, but cannot consistently connect growth to product margin, customer value, channel mix or campaign activity.

## Decisions supported

1. Where are revenue and gross profit growing or weakening?
2. Which categories and products combine scale with healthy margins?
3. Which channels and stores merit investigation or investment?
4. Which customer groups should enter measured retention or loyalty tests?
5. Which campaign associations warrant a controlled incrementality study?

## Users

- Commercial director: revenue, margin and channel mix.
- Category manager: assortment, price and discount review.
- Regional manager: store benchmarking within comparable regions.
- CRM analyst: customer segmentation and activation hypotheses.
- Finance partner: metric reconciliation and profitability controls.

## Success criteria

- Reproducible source-to-dashboard pipeline.
- Explicit KPI definitions and a constrained analytical model.
- Reconciled outputs usable in Python, SQL, Streamlit and Power BI.
- Decision language that distinguishes descriptive attribution from causal impact.
